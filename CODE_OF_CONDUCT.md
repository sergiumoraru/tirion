# Code of Conduct

Tirion welcomes contributors and users regardless of experience, background,
identity, or affiliation. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected Conduct

Be respectful, discuss technical disagreements with evidence, and give others
room to learn. Critique code and ideas without personal attacks. Respect privacy:
do not share private source, personal information, credentials, or conversations
without permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, deliberate
intimidation, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Do not retaliate against
someone for raising a concern or helping resolve one.

## Reporting And Enforcement

The responsible maintainer is [@sergiumoraru](https://github.com/sergiumoraru). Send conduct reports
privately, using the private contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not
describe an incident, or name the people involved, in a public issue, pull request,
or discussion.

Maintainers will consider the available evidence, respect requests for privacy,
and limit disclosure to what is necessary to investigate and respond. Responses
may include requesting a change in behavior, removing content, restricting project
participation, or banning an account. Serious threats or harassment may warrant
immediate restrictions. A participant may request reconsideration through the
same private channel and provide additional evidence.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
